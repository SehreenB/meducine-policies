Meducine — Request Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

Meducine gives you control over your personal data. You can request that some or all of the data associated with your account be deleted, whether or not you want to keep using the app.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to dev.meducine@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Meducine account (so we can verify the request is really you)
  2. In the subject line, write: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. In the body, tell us what you'd like deleted — either your entire account, or specific data only (see the table below for what can be deleted individually)
  4. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days

What Can Be Deleted

Data TypeCan Be Deleted Individually?
Full account (login, all associated data)Yes
NameYes

What Happens After Deletion

  • Requested data is permanently removed from our active database within 30 days of a verified request
  • If you delete your full account, you will be logged out and will need to create a new account to use Meducine again
  • Deleting individual data types (e.g., family health history) does not affect your ability to keep using your account — only that specific information is removed
  • Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is also deleted

Guest Mode Users

If you used Meducine without creating an account (Guest mode), your data is stored only on your device and was never sent to our servers. You can delete it at any time simply by uninstalling the app — no request needed.

Questions?

Contact us at dev.meducine@gmail.com for anything related to your data, including this deletion process.

See also our Privacy Policy for full details on what data we collect and how it's used.
