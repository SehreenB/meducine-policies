Meducine - Request Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

Meducine gives you control over your personal data. You can delete your Meducine account and associated personal information through the app, whether or not you want to continue using Meducine.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Meducine account through the account settings or account deletion options available within the Meducine application.

When you request account deletion:

  1. Your request will be processed, and your account will be scheduled for deletion.
  2. We will take reasonable steps to verify that the request is associated with your account.
  3. Personal information associated with your account will be removed from our active systems within 30 days of a verified deletion request, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law.
  4. Once your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to access your account or associated learning progress.

What Can Be Deleted

Data Type Can Be Deleted Individually?
Full account (login and all associated personal data) Yes
Name Yes, where account settings support this
Age range, biological sex, family health history Yes, where account settings support this
Learning progress (completed lessons, quiz scores, streaks, XP, badges) Yes
Bookmarked lessons Yes
Email address Only as part of full account deletion, as it may be required for account login

Where individual data controls are available within the Meducine application, you may remove optional information without deleting your entire account.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Personal information associated with your account will be removed from our active database within 30 days of a verified deletion request.
  • If you delete your full account, you will be logged out and will need to create a new account to use account-based features again.
  • Your account-based learning progress, including completed lessons, quiz scores, streaks, XP, badges, and bookmarks, will be deleted or anonymized as applicable.
  • Deleting individual optional information, such as family health history, does not affect your ability to continue using your account or Meducine's general educational content.
  • Some information may be retained for a limited period where required or permitted for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Such information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • Information stored in backups may take additional time to be fully removed in accordance with our backup and security processes.

Guest Mode Users

If you use Meducine without creating an account through Guest mode, your learning progress is stored locally on your device and is not associated with a Meducine account or directly linked to your identity.

You can generally delete Guest mode data by uninstalling the Meducine application or clearing its application data, depending on your device settings. No account deletion request is required.

Questions?

If you have questions about your personal data, account deletion, or Meducine's privacy practices, contact us at dev.meducine@gmail.com.

See also our Privacy Policy for full details about the information Meducine collects, how it is used, how it may be shared, and your privacy choices.
