Meducine Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Meducine ("we," "our," or "us") provides free, offline-friendly medical education content. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Meducine mobile app, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for login and account recovery
  • Full name — displayed within the app
  • Password — stored securely by our authentication provider (Supabase); we never see or store your plain-text password ourselves

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, in place of a separately created password.

You may also use Meducine as a Guest without creating an account. In this case, your progress is stored only on your device and is not linked to any personal information.

Onboarding & Learning Preferences

To personalize your learning experience, we may ask for:

  • Preferred language
  • Reading/learning level (Beginner, General Public, Health Student, Healthcare Professional)
  • Age range
  • Biological sex
  • Family health history categories (used only to suggest relevant lesson topics — never shared or sold)
  • General region
  • Notification preferences and reminder time
This information is optional context you can choose to share, is used solely to personalize which lessons are suggested to you, and is never sold or shared with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

Usage & Progress Data

  • Lessons completed, quiz scores, and reading levels used
  • Daily learning streaks and experience points (XP)
  • Bookmarked lessons

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your account and learning progress across devices
  • To personalize lesson recommendations
  • To track streaks, badges, and other learning-motivation features
  • To translate lesson content and app text into your selected language (via Google Cloud Translation API — the text being translated is sent to Google for this purpose; your personal account information is not included in these requests)
  • To send optional reminder notifications, if you've enabled them

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers, and we do not display ads in the Meducine app. Your data is processed only by the service providers listed in Section 3 above (Supabase, Sanity, Google Cloud Translation, and Google Sign-In), strictly to operate the app's core functionality on our behalf. We do not disclose your personal data to any independent third party for their own separate purposes.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Guest mode data stored only on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Meducine is intended for general audiences, including students learning medical concepts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (editable directly in-app under Profile settings)
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data (e.g., family health history) at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
