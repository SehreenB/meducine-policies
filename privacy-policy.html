Meducine Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026
Last updated: September 2026

Meducine Inc. ("Meducine," "we," "our," or "us") is a health education and health literacy platform that provides educational content, personalized learning experiences, and related features through the Meducine mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you when you use Meducine.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address (used for login, account management, and account recovery)
  • Name (may be displayed within the app)
  • Password (managed through Supabase; Meducine does not have access to your plain-text password)

If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google. Meducine does not receive or store your Google password.

If you use Guest mode, your learning progress is stored locally on your device and is not tied to an account or your identity. This information is removed when you delete the app or clear the app's data.

Onboarding and Learning Information

During onboarding, you may optionally and voluntarily provide:

  • Preferred language
  • Learning level (Beginner, General Public, Health Student, Healthcare Professional)
  • Age range
  • Biological sex
  • Family health history categories
  • General region
  • Notification preferences and reminder settings

This information is optional and is used to personalize educational content and lesson recommendations based on the information you choose to provide.

Health-Related Information

Some information you voluntarily provide during onboarding, such as biological sex and family health history categories, may be considered health-related or sensitive information.

This information is used only to help personalize health education and suggest relevant educational topics. Personalized recommendations are intended for educational purposes only. They are not medical diagnoses, medical risk assessments, treatment recommendations, or individualized medical advice.

Meducine does not intentionally collect medical records, clinical notes, laboratory results, or other records from your healthcare providers through the standard onboarding experience.

We do not sell health-related information or use it for targeted advertising.

Learning and Progress Data

  • Lessons viewed and completed
  • Quiz responses and scores
  • Learning level and progress
  • Daily learning streaks
  • XP, badges, and achievements
  • Bookmarked or saved lessons
  • Other information necessary for learning and personalization features

Device and Technical Information

We collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the application, such as app version, operating system, device information, and crash or diagnostic data.

Meducine does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, or other device information unrelated to the app's functionality unless we specifically notify you and obtain any required permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and maintain your learning progress across devices
  • Personalize lesson and content recommendations
  • Provide learning levels and educational pathways
  • Track streaks, XP, badges, and other motivation features
  • Provide content in your selected language
  • Translate lesson content and app text when requested
  • Send optional learning reminders and notifications
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, misuse, security, and technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Translation Services

When you request content in a language that requires translation, text submitted for translation may be sent to the Google Cloud Translation API.

Meducine does not intentionally include your name, email address, password, family health history, or other account information in translation requests.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with the following third-party service providers to operate Meducine:

Service Purpose Information Involved
Supabase Authentication and account/progress storage Email, name, learning preferences, and learning progress
Sanity.io Delivery of lesson and educational content Lesson and content data
Google Cloud Translation API Translation of lesson and app text Text submitted for translation
Google Sign-In Optional account creation and login Information provided by Google, such as name, email, and profile picture

We may add or change service providers as Meducine's technology and functionality develop. When third-party providers process information on our behalf, they are expected to handle that information in accordance with applicable contractual and legal requirements.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of services you choose to use.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising.

We may share or provide access to information to service providers that process information on our behalf when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or provide Meducine's functionality, as described in this Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with law, regulation, legal process, or lawful government requests
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Meducine, our users, or others
  • Detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Establish or defend legal claims
  • Support a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of business or assets

We do not disclose your personal information to independent third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

5. International Data Processing

Some of our service providers may process or store information outside Canada, including in the United States or other jurisdictions.

When information is processed outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of the jurisdiction in which it is processed. We take reasonable steps to work with service providers that maintain appropriate safeguards for information they process on our behalf.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Account and learning data are stored using third-party infrastructure providers described in this Privacy Policy.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Meducine's services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.

When an account is deleted through the Meducine application, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize personal information associated with that account, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law.

Information retained in backups may take additional time to be removed in accordance with our backup and security processes.

Guest-mode information stored locally on your device is generally removed when you uninstall the application or clear its application data, depending on your device settings.

8. Account Deletion

Registered users can delete their Meducine account through the account settings or account deletion options available within the Meducine application.

Deleting your account will initiate the removal of personal information associated with your account, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law. Uninstalling the Meducine application does not necessarily delete a registered Meducine account.

9. Children's Privacy

Meducine is intended for a general audience, including students and individuals interested in learning about health and medicine.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Meducine without appropriate consent, please contact us at meducineHQ@gmail.com.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include:

  • Requesting access to your personal information
  • Requesting correction of your personal information
  • Requesting deletion of your account and personal information
  • Withdrawing consent for optional information where applicable
  • Asking questions about how your information is collected and used
  • Making a privacy complaint to the appropriate privacy regulator

You can update certain information directly through your Meducine profile settings where those controls are available.

11. Withdrawing Optional Information

You may choose not to provide optional onboarding information.

Where available, you may also update or remove optional information, such as family health history, through your profile settings.

Withdrawing optional information may affect the personalization features available to you, but it will not prevent you from using Meducine's general educational content where those features do not require the information.

12. Medical Education Disclaimer

Meducine provides health and medical education for informational and educational purposes only.

Content available through Meducine is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Personalized lesson recommendations are intended to support health education and are not medical recommendations, diagnoses, risk assessments, or treatment plans.

You should not rely on Meducine to make decisions about your individual health or delay seeking care from a qualified healthcare professional.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or seek immediate medical attention.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Meducine's services, technology, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. Where appropriate, we may provide additional notice through the application, our website, or other reasonable means.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.
